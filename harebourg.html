How does Count Harebourg work?

Count Harebourg is the boss of the Clepsydra Tower in Frigost 3, in Dofus. Every turn, your spells are redirected by a rotation (90° clockwise, 90° counter-clockwise or 180°) around your character, depending on your vitality at the start of your turn.

This simulator tells you which cell to hit: pick your vitality range, place your character and the monster on the map, and the target cell shows up in blue. You get a warning when there is no line of sight or the target cell is off the map.
